Marine Cargo Insurance

Marine Cargo Insurance

Protects the cargo being transported against damage or loss during transit by sea, air, or land. It covers various perils, including damage caused by accidents, theft, fire, and natural disasters.

Overview

What is Marine Cargo Insurance?

Insurance that covers losses from damage or loss of goods during transit by land, air, or sea.

Understanding ICC

Differences Between ICC "A", "B" and "C"

Cargo insurance policies generally come in 3 types of coverage, commonly referred to as ICC "A", ICC "B" and ICC "C". ICC stands for Institute Cargo Clauses, which is the title of the clause/condition that governs the limits and conditions applied in a cargo shipping insurance policy.

A17 of 17 risks covered

Institute Cargo Clause A

All Risks — The most comprehensive coverage, covering all risks except those expressly excluded in the policy.

B12 of 17 risks covered

Institute Cargo Clause B

Named Perils (Broad) — Coverage based on specifically named risks, medium scope.

C7 of 17 risks covered

Institute Cargo Clause C

Named Perils (Basic) — The most basic coverage, only covering major risks.
